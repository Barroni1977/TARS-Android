TARS-AI — YOUR ONBOARD AI ASSISTANT

TARS-AI combines a distinctive metallic command-deck interface with local language models. Download a compatible GGUF model and run ordinary text chat directly on your phone.

WHAT YOU CAN DO

• Chat offline with an installed language model.
• Download or import models and choose one that fits your device.
• Manage separate AI profiles for different tasks.
• Explore technical questions, coding, writing and creative work.
• Monitor device and model performance.
• Enjoy the TARS-style animated startup interface.

LOCAL-FIRST, NOT MAGIC

Once installed, local chat inference does not need a cloud AI server. However, downloading models and certain optional features such as internet search, remote servers, voice providers, accounts and marketplace services require a network connection. Only enable services you want to use.

Device RAM, storage, model quality and processing speed vary. Small local models can make mistakes; always check important claims.

DEVELOPMENT STATUS

This is a TARS-AI development fork of the open-source PocketPal AI software. It is not affiliated with the film Interstellar. Its animated personality percentages are visual design elements while behavioural controls and dedicated offline speech are developed.
